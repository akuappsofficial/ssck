SSCK / KANDY / 1940—NOW
1940

THE BEGINNING

The College opened on 1 November 1940. Rev. Fr. D. Robert M. Perera served as its first principal, with the school beginning with about 250 students and 12 tutors.

1940—49

THE FIRST ERA

Fr. Robert Perera led the school through its early formative years before continuing as Rector from 1949 until his death in 1956.

1958

THE BABES

The annual cricket encounter with Vidyartha College began in 1958. The tradition became known as the Battle of the Babes.

NOW

THE NEXT ERA

Today's Sylvestrian identity extends beyond classrooms into sport, student media, coding, workshops, competitions and digital communities.

01NATIONAL SCHOOL

05THE ANTHEM

Dana Mana Ranjitha became the school anthem in the 1970s after earlier anthems were used.

06FOUNDER'S DAY

The annual Founder’s Day commemorates the origins of the College and its founding figures.

07KANDY

The campus sits on Yatinuwara Veediya, in the historic hill capital.

08THE COLORS
