01 / THE COLLEGE
NOT A MUSEUM.
A LIVING SCHOOL.

St. Sylvester's College is a national boys' school in Kandy, established on 1 November 1940. Its identity brings together Catholic and Benedictine heritage, education, competition, student life and generations of Sylvestrians.
