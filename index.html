01 — The College
A Kandy institution shaped by faith, education, sport and generations of Sylvestrians.

St. Sylvester's College began as a separate institution on 1 November 1940. The college is a national boys' school in the heart of Kandy, with Sinhala, Tamil and English education from primary through secondary levels.

02 — Heritage Mark

The crest is a map of the College's identity.

The Crown

Royalty and the highest authority associated with the country's historical context at the school's inception.

The Cross

The maroon cross represents the Roman Catholic faith on which the school was built.

The Book

The Holy Bible and education, carrying the words “Fiat Justitia”.

Cloud & Rays

Heavenly blessings and the Holy Spirit.

The Mountains

Kandy, the hill capital.

The Wand

The wand of St. Sylvester and the authority of the Sylvestro-Benedictine Congregation.

Trees & Bees

The school and the students and Old Boys who grow from it and contribute to it.

The Scroll

The College motto: Fortiter In Re.
